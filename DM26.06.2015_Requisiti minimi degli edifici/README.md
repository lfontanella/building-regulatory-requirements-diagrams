# D.M. 26 giugno 2015 – Requisiti minimi degli edifici

This folder contains BPMN-style diagrams related to the structuring of selected requirements from the Italian decree on minimum building energy performance requirements.

The diagrams were developed as part of a PhD research project on BIM, openBIM standards and digital procedures for building performance analysis.

## Diagram description

The diagram focuses on:

```text
D.M. 26 giugno 2015
Requisiti minimi degli edifici
Nuova costruzione
```

The aim is to represent selected energy-performance and building-envelope requirements as a structured workflow.

The diagram supports the interpretation of requirement logic related to:

- building intervention type;
- building envelope;
- thermal systems;
- climate zones;
- opaque and transparent envelope elements;
- thermal transmittance limits;
- energy performance indicators;
- digital structuring of regulatory checks.

## Role in the research workflow

This diagram supports the conversion of selected regulatory requirements into a visual and structured workflow.

It is connected to the broader research activities related to:

- BIM/openBIM-based building performance analysis;
- thermal transmittance checks;
- JSON-based requirement structuring;
- IDS-oriented information requirements;
- computational checking workflows;
- visual interpretation of regulatory logic.

The diagram helps identify which information may be required from a BIM/openBIM model in order to support energy-performance assessment workflows.

## Methodological note

The diagram is a research interpretation of selected requirements from the decree.

It is not an official legal interpretation and should not be considered a certified compliance tool.

For any professional, legal or operational use, requirements must be verified against the official regulatory text and any applicable updates.

## Limitations

This folder does not include:

- complete legal text;
- official regulatory interpretation;
- certified compliance procedures;
- building models;
- project-specific data.

The diagram is provided as methodological and research documentation.
