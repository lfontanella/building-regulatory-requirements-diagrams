# Building Regulatory Requirements Diagrams

This repository contains visual diagrams developed as part of a PhD research project on BIM, openBIM standards and digital procedures for building performance analysis.

The repository documents the translation of selected building regulatory requirements into structured visual workflows and BPMN-style diagrams.

The diagrams are intended to support the understanding, organization and digital interpretation of regulatory and information requirements in BIM/openBIM workflows.

## Research context

This repository is part of the digital companion material of the PhD thesis:

**Utilizzo della metodologia BIM (Building Information Modeling) e degli standard openBIM per le analisi e il monitoraggio delle prestazioni termiche di edifici storici**

The research investigates how BIM, HBIM, openBIM standards and data-driven workflows can support:

- building performance analysis;
- information requirement structuring;
- regulatory requirement interpretation;
- energy and environmental performance workflows;
- translation of normative rules into digital procedures;
- support to BIM/openBIM-based decision-making processes.
