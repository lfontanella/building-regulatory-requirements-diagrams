# Building Regulatory Requirements Diagrams

This repository contains visual diagrams developed as part of a PhD research project on BIM, openBIM standards and digital procedures for building performance analysis.

The repository documents the translation of selected building regulatory requirements into structured visual workflows and BPMN-style diagrams.

The diagrams are intended to support the understanding, organization and digital interpretation of regulatory and information requirements in BIM/openBIM workflows.

## Research context

This repository is part of the digital companion material of the PhD thesis:

**Utilizzo della metodologia BIM (Building Information Modeling) e degli standard openBIM per le analisi e il monitoraggio delle prestazioni termiche di edifici storici**

The research investigates how BIM, HBIM, openBIM standards and data-driven workflows can support:

- building performance analysis;
- information requirement structuring;
- regulatory requirement interpretation;
- energy and environmental performance workflows;
- translation of normative rules into digital procedures;
- support to BIM/openBIM-based decision-making processes.

## Contents

The repository currently includes two groups of diagrams:

1. diagrams related to the **CAM – Criteri Ambientali Minimi**;
2. diagrams related to the **D.M. 26 giugno 2015 – Requisiti minimi degli edifici**.

Each group contains both the editable BPMN source file and the exported PNG image.

## CAM – Criteri Ambientali Minimi

The folder:

```text
CAM_Criteri Ambientali Minimi/
```

contains diagrams related to the structuring of selected requirements from the CAM framework.

The included material focuses on the organization of environmental and technical requirements into a visual workflow structure.

The objective is to support the interpretation of regulatory requirements in relation to BIM/openBIM information workflows.

## D.M. 26 giugno 2015 – Requisiti minimi degli edifici

The folder:

```text
DM26.06.2015_Requisiti minimi degli edifici/
```

contains diagrams related to selected requirements from the Italian minimum building requirements decree.

The included material focuses on the visual organization of energy-performance and building-envelope requirements, with particular attention to the workflow logic needed for digital interpretation and BIM/openBIM implementation.

## File types

Each diagram is provided in two formats:

- `.bpmn` — editable BPMN/source file;
- `.png` — exported image for documentation and visual consultation.

The BPMN files represent the editable source diagrams, while the PNG files are intended for direct viewing, presentation and inclusion in documentation.

## Role in the research workflow

The diagrams support the broader research workflow by helping to translate regulatory and technical requirements into structured digital procedures.

They are connected to the development of:

- BIM/openBIM information requirements;
- IDS-oriented checking procedures;
- JSON-based requirement structuring;
- computational workflows for building performance analysis;
- visual representations of regulatory logic.

The aim is not to reproduce legal texts, but to support the interpretation and organization of regulatory requirements in a form that can be more easily connected to digital workflows.

## Methodological purpose

The diagrams were developed to make complex regulatory structures more readable and operational from a BIM/openBIM perspective.

They can support:

- identification of relevant requirement categories;
- mapping of decision paths;
- understanding of dependencies between requirements;
- preparation of information requirements;
- development of computational checking workflows;
- comparison between regulatory logic and model-based data structures.

## Data and publication note

This repository contains diagrams and visual documentation only.

It does not include:

- confidential building models;
- private project data;
- personal data;
- credentials;
- proprietary BIM models;
- complete legal or regulatory texts.

The diagrams are research interpretations and should not be considered official legal documents.

## Limitations

The diagrams are research outputs and methodological visualizations.

They are not intended as:

- official legal interpretations;
- certified regulatory compliance tools;
- substitutes for the original laws, decrees, standards or guidelines;
- professional legal or technical advice;
- complete reproductions of the original regulatory documents.

Any professional or operational use must be verified against the official applicable regulatory sources.
