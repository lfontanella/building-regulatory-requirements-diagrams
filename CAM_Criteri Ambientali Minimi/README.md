# CAM – Criteri Ambientali Minimi

This folder contains BPMN-style diagrams related to the structuring of selected requirements from the Italian CAM framework.

The diagrams were developed as part of a PhD research project on BIM, openBIM standards and digital procedures for building performance analysis.

## Diagram description

The diagram focuses on:

```text
D.M. 23 giugno 2022 – CAM
Section 2.4 – Specifiche tecniche progettuali per gli edifici
```

The aim is to translate selected environmental and technical requirements into a structured visual workflow.

The diagram supports the identification of:

- requirement categories;
- decision paths;
- relationships between environmental criteria and building design choices;
- possible information requirements for BIM/openBIM workflows;
- digital interpretation of regulatory logic.

## Role in the research workflow

This diagram supports the translation of environmental regulatory requirements into a form that can be connected to BIM/openBIM information workflows.

It can be used as a visual support for:

- understanding CAM requirement structures;
- mapping information needs;
- identifying relevant building-related data;
- preparing structured information requirements;
- supporting digital checking or semi-automated interpretation workflows.

## Methodological note

The diagram is a research interpretation of selected regulatory requirements.

It is not an official version of the CAM requirements and should not be used as a substitute for the original regulatory document.

For any professional or operational use, the diagram must be checked against the official CAM text and the applicable regulatory framework.

## Limitations

This folder does not include:

- complete legal text;
- official regulatory interpretation;
- certified compliance procedures;
- building models;
- project-specific data.

The diagram is provided as methodological and research documentation.
